BAY 09 / FAQ

Questions buyers
ask us first.

Still unsure? Talk to a founder — not a salesperson.

Who owns the code and design files?

You do — 100%. Repos live in your GitHub or GitLab from day one, and all Figma files, credentials and documentation are handed over at launch.

Can you work inside our existing stack?

Yes. We regularly work in Next.js, React, Node, Python, Laravel, Webflow, HubSpot and Zoho. If your stack is unusual, we'll tell you honestly in the discovery call.

How does white-labelling work for agencies?

We sign an NDA and non-solicit, use your email domain and tools, and join your client calls only if you want us there. Your client never sees the Loadbay name.

What if the scope changes mid-project?

Changes go through a short written change request with cost and timeline impact. Nothing is billed without your sign-off.

Do you work with early, pre-revenue startups?

Yes — many of our best builds started at pre-seed. We'll help you cut the MVP to what proves the business, so budget goes into what matters.
